O﻿n review sites, especially those for books, ratings tend to be set to stars, with five stars being \*perfection\*. And while this might be fine for objective rankings based on imperical evidence, opinions are not definitive, but based on personal preference. Which is why, to remind reviewers and readers that these are subjective, books should be ranked with hearts instead of stars.

I﻿ have been using heart rankings in my book journal for just over a year now. When I set up my A6 notebook, I made a little key to remind myself what my standards to ensure consistency. One heart was a DNF or hating it; two was a book I disliked, or just felt was forgettable; three meant it was just ok; four if I liked it and would reread it (which I do often); and five was for books I loved and new favourites.
